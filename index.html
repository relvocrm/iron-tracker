Access

Iron Log runs entirely on this device, so this is a soft access switch — not real account security. Anyone with access to the app or its browser storage could bypass it. Use it to keep day-to-day staff use focused, not to protect sensitive data from a determined user.

Admin PIN — required to switch back from Staff to Admin mode. Leave blank for no PIN.

Back up your data

Everything you log — gym sessions, business entries, family events, and more — is saved on this device only. Export a backup regularly so a lost or broken phone never costs you your history.

Save it to Google Drive

  1. Tap "Export backup" above — the file downloads to your phone.
  2. Open the Google Drive app, tap the + button, then "Upload".
  3. Select the downloaded file (named like iron-log-backup-2026-08-16.json).
  4. It's now safely stored in your Drive, separate from your phone.
Restore data

Importing a file completely replaces everything currently on this device — old categories, sessions, or entries are wiped first so nothing from before can reappear. Backups from the older gym-only version of Iron Log also import fine — they'll land in your Gym / Fitness area.
